About Me

I am affiliated with Nanjing University. My research interests lie in computer vision and generative modeling, especially video diffusion, streaming generation, human animation, identity editing, diffusion distillation, and photorealistic animatable avatars.

Research Interests

  • Generative video models, diffusion transformers, and streaming inference.
  • Human animation, audio-visual identity editing, and controllable video generation.
  • Animatable avatars, temporal cloth dynamics, and on-policy diffusion distillation.
